Allen Wyma talks with [Jonas Kruckenberg](https://jonaskruckenberg.de/) from [Mainmatter](https://mainmatter.com/) about migrating production C and C++ codebases to Rust and the [C to Rust Migration Book](https://mainmatter.com/c-to-rust-migration-book/). They discuss incremental migration, refactoring legacy code, designing safe FFI boundaries, and testing undefined behavior.
